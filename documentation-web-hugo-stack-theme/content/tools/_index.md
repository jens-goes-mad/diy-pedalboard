# Tools of the Trade — Beyond Code
Building a DIY pedal board isn’t just about code and circuits — it’s about navigating a jungle of tools, gadgets, 
and software that, at first glance, have little to do with development. 
But here's the secret: the right tools make the impossible manageable.

In this chapter, I’ll walk you through the tools I used — from unexpected software gems to good old screwdrivers 
and share why they worked for me. It’s not about following trends or using what’s popular. 
It’s about finding what fits your brain, your hands, and your workflow.

Use tools whenever possible. Choose what works. Automate what you can. 
And don’t be afraid to bend the rules to make things easier.
